Reared, not just resold

A herd we know animal by animal.

Husbandry practices that keep a herd healthy and worth buying.

Cattle farming rewards patience — steady feeding, proper husbandry, and knowing your herd well enough to spot a problem before it becomes one. That's the standard we hold our operation to, and it's why buyers who've worked with us once tend to come back when they're ready to expand a herd or restock.

Whether you're buying your first few head, expanding an existing herd, or looking to sell cattle you've raised, we deal directly and explain condition, weight and price plainly before any money changes hands.
