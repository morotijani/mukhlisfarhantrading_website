Our story

Four trades,
one standard.

Built in the Ashanti goldfields, run on trust.

Mukhlis Farhan Trading Limited was established in 2018 in Konongo, in the heart of Ghana's Ashanti Region — a town shaped by generations of mining and farming. That inheritance is the reason the business trades in four directions at once: equipment for the pits and quarries, machinery for the farm, cattle raised and sold with care, and gold bought and weighed with an honest scale.

What ties the four together isn't convenience — it's the belief that a trading house earns its name one fair transaction at a time. Whether a client is outfitting a small-scale mining site, stocking a farm ahead of planting season, expanding a herd, or bringing gold to be valued, they deal with the same people, the same standards, and the same word kept.
