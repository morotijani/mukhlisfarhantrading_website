Built for the pit, not the shelf

Equipment that earns its keep.

Sourced for sites that can't afford downtime.

Small-scale mining runs on equipment that has to survive red laterite dust, river water and long hours — not equipment built for a showroom. We stock and source digging tools, processing equipment and safety gear with that reality in mind, and we're straightforward about what a piece of kit can and can't do before it leaves our yard.

Most orders come from operators we've worked with for years, but we take on new sites too. If you're setting up a claim, replacing worn gear, or need materials on short notice, tell us the job and we'll tell you honestly what fits it.

Excavation & digging tools

Shovels, mattocks, chisels and hand-digging equipment sized for artisanal and small-scale sites.

Processing & washing equipment

Sluices, pans, screens and washing plant components for separating ore from waste on-site.

Safety gear & consumables

Boots, gloves, helmets and the day-to-day consumables a site burns through without noticing.

Site materials on order

Not in stock doesn't mean not available — we source materials against a site's order sheet.
