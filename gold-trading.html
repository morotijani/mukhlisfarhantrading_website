Where trust matters most

Every gram accounted for.

No back-room offers — the scale does the talking.

Gold trading is the division where a trading house's reputation is made or lost fastest, so it's the one we're most exact about. Every piece brought to us is weighed and checked for purity in front of the seller, and every offer we make is set against the day's gold price rather than negotiated in the dark.

We buy from small-scale miners, individuals and other sellers, and we sell to registered buyers. Whichever side of the counter you're on, you leave with paperwork that records exactly what happened — weight, purity, price and date.

Walk-in gold evaluation

Bring gold in and have it weighed and purity-checked on the spot, no appointment required.

Purchase at verified weight & purity

Offers are calculated against verified weight, purity and the current day's gold price.

Sale to registered buyers

Gold we hold is sold on to registered, licensed buyers through a documented chain.

Documentation on every deal

Buying or selling, you leave with a written record of weight, purity, price and date.

How a deal works

Three steps, nothing hidden.

Step 01 — Weigh & testYour gold is weighed and purity-tested in front of you, with the result explained plainly.
Step 02 — Agree a priceThe offer is calculated against the day's market price — you'll see the numbers, not just the total.
Step 03 — Settle & documentPayment is settled on the spot, and you leave with paperwork recording the whole transaction.
